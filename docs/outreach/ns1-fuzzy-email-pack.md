# NS-1 — EuroDigital fuzzy-variable email pack

DRAFTS ONLY. Human-use copy; no sending system, prospect list, automation, or CRM wiring. David sends individually; Jack may send only with David's approval for each send. All example businesses and their facts below are fictional. EuroDigital is the sender, not an example prospect.

## Usage in under five minutes

1. Spend the first minute on the business's own site and public listing. Pick one offer: a new build when the contact path or site structure needs work; a chatbot when published answers are hard to find; content repurposing when existing posts or review themes could support useful site copy.
2. Spend the next two minutes checking two or three relevant observations from the catalogue. Each lookup below should take about two minutes or less on its own; do not attempt the whole catalogue. Record the page URL, date, and exact visible evidence in a private per-message draft, not a prospect list. Do not submit forms, run crawlers, collect personal details, or infer customer losses.
3. Use the remaining time to fill the chosen body, check role relevance and consent, choose a subject, and append the completed identification/unsubscribe block. Read it aloud. Every observation must support the proposed work; deleting the observations should destroy the specific rationale. If a published fact is ambiguous, describe only what you saw or skip it.

**Kill rule:** if the variables cannot be filled truthfully in five minutes, skip the prospect. Fake personalization is worse than no message. An absent button on one page is not proof that the entire business has no booking system. Do not claim measured speed, lost revenue, or demand without evidence. Confirm consent separately before any future human send; this pack authorizes no sending.

Braces contain observations, not names or mail-merge greetings. Fill them as the grammatical phrases shown below. Initial bodies have exactly three fuzzy variables. Body word counts exclude subjects and the mandatory footer; recheck after filling. Keep initial bodies at 90–140 words and first follow-ups at 40–70 words by shortening observations without changing their meaning.

## Human follow-up rules

The offsets are calendar days after the initial email, not automatic timers or days after the previous follow-up. They are optional review points, not permission to send. Recheck the fact and consent before each message; follow-up 1 introduces a new observation, not a recycled opening. Skip any follow-up whose observation cannot be verified. Each +7-day message is the free one-paragraph observation itself, with no meeting or purchase request. Append the footer to every message, including the break-up.

Stop the sequence when the recipient replies; handle their response individually. Stop immediately on refusal or unsubscribe, and send nothing further after the break-up unless they initiate contact. Silence is not consent. These drafts are never connected to a scheduler, mailing list, or sending platform.

## CASL compliance skeleton

**legal-shape checklist, NOT legal advice**

Template prepared 2026-11-13; not legal advice; David confirms before first use.

- Confirm and document the consent basis before any human send. A conspicuously published business email may support implied consent only when publication has no restriction against unsolicited commercial messages and the message relates to the recipient's business role or duties. Publication alone is not blanket permission; B2B relevance alone is not an exemption. Do not guess addresses or harvest personal data. [CRTC implied-consent guidance](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm).
- Use express consent where required; if no valid basis is established, do not send. Preserve the source, date, relevance, and consent evidence for the individual message outside this repository. [Government consent guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email).
- Complete the identification block below, including the valid marketing-footer mailing address. If Jack sends, identify Jack by full name as the actual sender on behalf of David Heslop / EuroDigital. Do not send with unresolved placeholders.
- Use a monitored reply address and verify reply-based unsubscribe works before first use. Keep contact information and the unsubscribe mechanism valid for at least 60 days after sending. Honour requests without delay and within 10 business days, at no cost; this workflow stops further contact immediately. [CRTC FAQs](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm).
- Standing rule: one-at-a-time human sends to named businesses only, never bulk, never purchased lists. David sends, or Jack sends with David's approval for each message. No prospect list is part of this pack.
