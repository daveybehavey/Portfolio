**[PROPOSED — not published, needs David's approval]** Apply the Saraev pricing principles conservatively: show Core as the normal choice for the intended buyer, keep Starter useful and independently deliverable, and reserve Plus for a real additional need. No artificial decoys or promised commercial results. These are project starting points; scope, dependencies and final price belong in the written proposal.

**[PROPOSED — not published, needs David's approval]** A revision round means consolidated buyer feedback within approved scope, not new pages, a new concept or added source material.
