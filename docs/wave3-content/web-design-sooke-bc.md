## Proposed page copy

# Make your working area easier to understand

A business can serve Sooke without having a shop customers can visit. Its website needs to make both facts clear: where the work is available and whether there is a public location. That distinction is a useful starting point for a service website west of Victoria.

EuroDigital operates as a service-area studio from Victoria, with no Sooke storefront. Projects run by message and call, with a written scope and review before launch. Proximity does not imply a walk-in office or a promise of on-site appointments.

## Separate your base from your coverage

Start with three questions: where is the business based, where can customers receive the service, and is there a location they should visit? The answers may differ. A contact section should help someone act on those answers without suggesting an address is open to the public when it is not.

If your work covers East Sooke, western communities, or parts of the wider capital region, name only the places you actually serve. These are possible coverage choices for you to confirm, not a list every Sooke business should adopt.

## Give uncertain locations a next step

An area list has a boundary. Someone just beyond it still needs to know how to ask whether their job is suitable. Explain how to check coverage and which details to send. Avoid language that silently commits you to travelling anywhere or accepting every kind of work.

For example, a business might ask someone to describe the location and requested service before discussing a visit. That is a content-planning example, not a claim about your availability or a new service policy. The business owner supplies the actual rule.

## Match the site size to the explanation

A focused service may need only a concise description, clear coverage, and contact information. A business with several substantially different services may need room to explain each. Decide that from the material customers need rather than from a desire to list more places. Photos, credentials, and project descriptions must come from evidence you are entitled to publish.

### Does service-area language guarantee search visibility?

No. Accurate coverage helps describe the business; it does not establish rankings or replace a separately scoped search discussion. Business Profile support also depends on eligibility and agreed scope.
