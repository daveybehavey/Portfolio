## Proposed page copy

# A Victoria business website with the right work in scope

A website brief can start simply: explain the business and make it easy to get in touch. It becomes a different project when it also needs a booking tool, a migration, or a connection to another system. Getting that distinction clear before the build helps you compare proposals on the work they actually include.

I'm David Heslop, the person behind EuroDigital, a one-person studio based in Victoria. This is a service-area business rather than a staffed storefront. Start with your current website, the action you want visitors to take, and anything the business already relies on.

## Separate the message from the machinery

First, decide what a visitor needs to understand: the service, who it suits, relevant work, and how to ask about it. A focused offer may fit on one page. Several services may need their own explanations so the homepage does not have to carry every detail.

Then list the features. A link to an existing booking service and a custom booking flow require different work. Tell me which tools you use and what information needs to move between them. Custom integrations and migrations are quoted after discovery, with their assumptions and exclusions written down.

## Bring the material only you can verify

Your business details, actual coverage, photos you can use, and service notes are the starting material. Rough notes can begin the conversation. Credentials, project descriptions, and customer quotations need your verification and permission before they belong on a page. Copywriting and photography are separate scope decisions.

## Know what happens after the build

The proposal sets out pages, content responsibilities, setup work, review, and timing. Account access and launch notes belong in that conversation too. Domain and hosting connection, measurement tools, and Business Profile support follow the agreed scope; they are not a promise of search performance. Later additions need a defined piece of work rather than an assumed open-ended commitment.

Possibly. Send the URL and describe the problem. A narrow repair may be enough; an existing-site rebuild belongs in a [redesign discussion](/website-redesign-vancouver-island).
