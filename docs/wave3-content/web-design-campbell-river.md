## Proposed page copy

# Decide how the website project will work before it starts

Choosing a web designer also means choosing how you will share information, review work, and take ownership afterwards. For a Campbell River business considering EuroDigital, those practical questions deserve a direct answer: the studio is in Victoria, and the project runs remotely by message and call.

There is no Campbell River office. I have not yet built a website for a business here. If you need a designer based in Campbell River or regular on-site work, establish that requirement before considering a remote proposal.

## Check the working arrangement against your needs

Who in your business can supply service details and review pages? Can they give feedback remotely? Do you already have usable photos, or does the project need separately scoped photography? These questions matter because a remote build cannot supply first-hand knowledge of your premises or operations by assumption.

Share the way you prefer to make decisions and any constraints on availability. Review dependencies belong in the timeline. A date is only meaningful alongside the content and approvals needed to reach it.

## Treat ownership as part of the brief

Before the build, identify who controls your domain and hosting and what access can be provided. The scope should say which connections and setup work are included. At handoff, account ownership and launch notes should be understandable to the person who will look after the site.

Measurement tools and Business Profile support follow their agreed boundaries. They should not become an implied ongoing management service. If you need someone to maintain content or handle future additions, ask about that work explicitly rather than assume it comes with the launch.

## Review a customer task, then the handoff

Use a real service question from your business to review the proposed site: can a visitor understand the work, check coverage, and find the next step? Separately, review what you receive as the owner. A clear customer path and clear account access solve different problems; both need attention.
