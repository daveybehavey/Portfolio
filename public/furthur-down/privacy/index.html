Furthur Down privacy policy · Updated 26 November 2026

Furthur Down is published by EuroDigital. For privacy questions or support, email support@eurodigital.ca.

Current Android testing builds. Progress is stored on your device. Optional Google test ads and Play catalog/history checks contact Google. Real purchases and revenue-generating ads are not enabled. This notice describes testing builds with these features; we will update it before changing these practices.

Your progress and preferences

The game stores chapter earnings, upgrades, best distances, discoveries, completed chapters, stance, sound and control preferences, age group and privacy acknowledgement on your device. We do not ask for your full birth date. There is no game account or game-operated cloud-save service. The optional previous-run ghost lasts for the current session.

Testing versions also store simulated supporter ownership, outfit selection and a local test receipt. These are not payment records or proof of Google Play ownership. Clearing application data or uninstalling may remove progress and test receipts. We do not promise cloud backup or recovery; your operating system may have separate backup settings.

Optional Google services

The Android testing version includes Google Mobile Ads, Google's User Messaging Platform for consent choices, and Unity In-App Purchasing with Google Play Billing.

Adult testers who complete the age/privacy choice can explicitly request Google test rewarded ads. The game uses Google's sample ad placement, requests non-personalized ads and sets a General Audiences content rating. These test ads do not generate advertising revenue. Declining an ad does not remove ordinary ride earnings or prevent chapter completion.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), product interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads and test ads can still involve data processing. Read Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's privacy policy for details.

Adult testers can separately request a Google Play catalog and purchase-history check. This contacts Google Play. Real checkout is disabled: the game does not initiate a purchase or request payment-card details. The history check does not grant or restore ownership. The supporter purchase and restore buttons currently simulate ownership locally, without a charge.

Children and age choices

Children under 13 are included in the game's intended audience. The current testing version blocks its network test-ad and Play Billing-check entry points for under-18 and unknown-age profiles. A parent-facing entry protects the local cosmetic simulation; this is not a verified parental-consent process.

You may play without requesting ads or a Play Billing check. Age selection alone is not consent to every use of personal information. Before broader advertising or purchase features are enabled, we will update this notice and the applicable privacy choices.

Diagnostics and support

Developer tools may generate local logs, screenshots and recordings. Those tools do not automatically upload these files. Sharing them with a tester or developer is a separate action. There is no game-operated analytics backend, chat, multiplayer or player-location sharing. Third-party SDK processing is described above.

If you email support, your address, message and voluntarily supplied attachments are received by EuroDigital and its email providers to answer your request. Please do not send payment credentials or unnecessary information about a child.

This policy page is hosted by Cloudflare, which receives the technical information needed to deliver and protect the page, such as your IP address and browser request details. The page blocks analytics scripts. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for its information practices.

Retention, deletion and your controls

Game progress and test receipts remain on your device until removed. There is no game-operated server copy to delete or restore. On Android, open your phone's Settings, find Furthur Down under Apps, then open Storage and choose the option to clear the app's storage or data. Names vary by phone.

Clearing app storage deletes progress, preferences, age selection and the local test receipt. The game's Restore test pack button cannot undo that deletion. Do not clear storage just to install an update. Operating-system backups, if any, have separate controls.

You can review your age choice in the game's privacy settings. Google's privacy-options entry appears when required and lets eligible testers review their ad choices. Changing choices or clearing game data does not erase information already processed by Google. Google's privacy policy and account controls explain its retention and deletion practices.

EuroDigital deletes routine support emails, including attachments, within 90 days after resolving a request, except records needed for legal obligations or fraud matters. Contact support@eurodigital.ca to ask about access to or deletion of your support correspondence, including information supplied about a child. Support correspondence is separate from your local game save.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's information practices change and show the update date above. Real purchases, production advertising or a developer-operated verification service will require disclosures reflecting those features before they are enabled.
